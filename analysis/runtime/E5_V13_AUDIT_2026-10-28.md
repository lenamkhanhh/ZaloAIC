### Channel gap and definition reconciliation (no post-run parser change)

0653, both arms, first executes flights.search with a date; after empty results it emits exactly:

`sys.ask_missing_info tool_name=flights.search params=["date"]`

This is an unsubmitted pseudo-call, not a real ask. It lacks the registered opening parenthesis, so E6-C treats it as plain-text stop. This is a parser-scope gap discovered by the predeclared manual review. The trace already has one submitted read, so it is **not** the zero-submission SILENT_PSEUDO trace defined explicitly in the frozen pre-launch handoff c8a7d46 §1.5. Registered silent is therefore0 and the primary +1 verdict remains INCONCLUSIVE. Under a broader all-output reading of the brief's silent wording, these outputs would instead trigger CONFOUNDED; that sensitivity is disclosed, not silently substituted after seeing results. Do not claim universal channel repair or that all failed asks reflect only intent.

This gap does not change the measured first-call failure on0653 (a read was already first), but it limits statements about subsequent ask recovery. No parser widening, rerun or alternate scoring was applied. The two caught-failure control targets also limit pure intent attribution. The optional post-outcome channel-recovered-only subset proposal was not adopted; all7 targets remain in the denominator.
