## Why this is confirmed and what it does not establish

The frozen C criterion allowed either a recovered submitted tagged call **or explicit failure after retry**. V11 takes the second branch. It confirms detection/observability for the narrow registered lowercase app.tool( prefix; synthetic CPU tests also preserve normal prose and explicit stops. It does not establish detection of every possible pseudo-code spelling, prose-wrapped calls or fenced variants, and does not imply that all plain-text termination is correct. Parser scope was not broadened during the run.

The generic record status INFERRED means the runtime returned a trace; it does not mean format or task success. This audit explicitly marks 0116 as FORMAT_FAILURE_RETRY_EXHAUSTED. No extra fourth attempt is introduced to obtain a nicer result.
